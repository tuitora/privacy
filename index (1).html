Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 6 November 2026

Tuitora is a mobile application that helps students and parents in India find tutors and tuition centres near them, and helps tutors reach students. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to this Policy.

Operator of Tuitora: S. Kaarthikeyan, Bangalore, Karnataka, India ("Tuitora", "we", "us"). For the purposes of Indian data protection law, we are the party that decides how your personal information is used.

Summary

  • We collect only what the app needs: account details, tutor profiles, messages and, if you allow it, your location.
  • Your phone number and email address are not shown to other users. People contact each other through in-app chat.
  • Tutor verification documents are private. A government ID photo is deleted once the tutor is approved.
  • We do not sell your information and we do not show advertisements.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

2.How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, let you log in, and send password-reset codes by email.
  • To show students tutors that match their subject, class, budget and distance.
  • To let students and tutors message each other inside the app.
  • To check that tutors are genuine before they are shown to students.
  • To send alerts you have allowed, such as new messages and enquiries.
  • To respond to feedback and help requests, keep the app safe, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3.Consent

We use your information on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create an account and when you choose to share optional information, such as your location or a profile photo. You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 8). If you withdraw consent for something the app needs to work, such as your account details, we may no longer be able to provide the service to you.

4.Who can see your information

  • Students and parents can see a verified tutor's public profile: name, photo, bio, subjects, classes, fees, timings, general area, rating and reviews.
  • Tutors can see the name and messages of students who contact them.
  • Phone numbers and email addresses are not shown to other users.
  • Reviews show the name of the person who wrote them.
  • Verification documents can be opened only by the Tuitora team (see section 5).

5.Verification documents

We know these documents are personal, so we keep them to the minimum:

  • We ask for them only from tutors, and only to check that the tutor is who they say they are.
  • They are stored in private storage. Other users cannot see them.
  • If you use Aadhaar, please upload the masked copy, where only the last 4 digits are visible. Please do not upload a full Aadhaar number.
  • Your government ID photo is deleted when we approve your profile. We keep only the fact that you were verified.
  • Your education certificate and experience letter are kept while your account exists and are deleted when you delete your account.

8.Your rights and choices

  • Access: ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: edit your details in the app, or ask us to fix them.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app under Settings, then Delete Account, or ask us to do it.
  • Withdraw consent: you can turn off location and notifications at any time in your phone's settings or the app's Settings.
  • Complaints: write to the contact in section 13. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is in operation.

To use any of these rights, write to tuitorasupport@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as we reasonably can.

9.Children

Tuitora is meant for parents and guardians, and for students who use it with a parent's or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 18 without a parent's or guardian's consent. If you believe a child has given us information without permission, write to us and we will remove it.

10.Security

Information travels between the app and our servers over secure connections. Accounts and private documents are protected by access rules, so people can open only what they are allowed to. No system is completely safe, so please choose a strong password and never share your login or reset codes with anyone.

11.Storage outside India

Our service providers may store information on servers located outside India. We choose providers that protect information with security measures.

12.Changes to this Policy

If we change this Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the app.
